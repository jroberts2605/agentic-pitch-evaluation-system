## SnackPocket — Consumer Product

**Classification:** Consumer Product  
**Iterations:** 1  
**Result:** Passed

### Classification Reasoning

SnackPocket is a subscription box service delivering curated snack boxes directly to families with kids. The product is targeted at individual consumers (parents and kids), not businesses. There is no indication of proprietary technology, novel science, or B2B SaaS software. The core of the value proposition is in product curation, convenience, and marketing to parents through schools, fitting the definition of a consumer startup.

### Final Pitch

Last Tuesday, a mom in our pilot group texted a photo of her seven-year-old reading the SnackPocket ingredient label out loud at the breakfast table, sounding out each word. Parents tell us they want better snacks for their kids but get exhausted reading every label themselves at the grocery store. Last year, three major school districts in our region tightened their snack guidelines, suddenly raising the bar for what kids can bring to lunch—leaving parents scrambling for compliant, healthy options. SnackPocket curates monthly boxes built around real-food ingredients, each with a kid-friendly trading card, so parents can feel confident about what their kids are eating. We’re launching through three school PTA fundraisers next month—each fundraiser already reaches hundreds of families per school, giving us instant access to engaged parents and a built-in referral network.

### Final Evaluation

> This pitch clearly meets all three rubric criteria with a compelling hook, timely relevance, and a well-explained distribution plan.

**Rubric feedback:**

1. **Hook:** The pitch opens with a vivid, specific customer moment—a mom sharing a photo of her child engaging with the product label—which draws the audience in effectively.
2. **Why now:** The pitch clearly explains new stricter snack guidelines in schools as the recent change making the product timely and necessary for parents.
3. **Distribution:** The pitch specifies initial distribution via PTA fundraisers at schools and justifies this channel by highlighting immediate access to engaged parents and a strong referral network.
